Who you're working with

The Silent Monk is led by me, Jernej Zidar — an HPC specialist who has spent a career at the point where complex compute systems meet the messy reality of production.

As a technology consultant at Hewlett Packard Enterprise, I deploy, configure, and stabilise large-scale HPC systems in customer environments. I've taken part in the late-stage commissioning of flagship GPU-based supercomputers — coordinated work across tens of cabinets and thousands of accelerators, among the largest such systems in Europe.

Before HPE, I spent more than a decade in Singapore's HPC community — spanning national supercomputing infrastructure, HPC benchmarking on-premise and in the cloud, and A*STAR research computing. It's underpinned by a PhD in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, where I used HPC to study amyloid-beta and lipid raft formation — giving me a genuine fluency with the scientific workloads these systems exist to run.

That combination — vendor-grade deployment experience, research-side understanding, and years of hands-on operations — is exactly what I bring to every engagement.
